Black Friday is Friday, November 27, 2026. This is not a list of everything on sale. It is a short list of things we already recommend for older parents, so if one of them drops in price, you know it is worth buying.

Holiday visits are often when families notice something has changed: Dad has the TV louder than before, Mom is not answering the door, or a parent who lives alone has had a scare. Black Friday lands right in the middle of that season, and the sales are loud. Most of what is advertised is not useful for an older parent, and a "50% off" sticker means little if the list price was never real.

So this guide works the other way round. Every pick below comes from one of our full reviews, where you can read the details. For each one we give the usual price, taken from the maker's own page or from our monthly price check, so you can compare it with whatever price you see on Black Friday. We do not list deal prices, because they change by the hour; the Check Price buttons show you the current one.

The list starts with safety, because a medical alert system is the purchase families most often make after a holiday visit, then moves through tablets, doorbells and cameras, and ends with TV and hearing help.

Quick tip: Several picks here are Amazon devices (Fire tablets, Fire TV, Ring and Blink). If your parent doesn't have Prime, start a free trial if they are eligible.

How to Tell a Real Black Friday Deal

1. Compare with the usual price, not the "was" price

The crossed-out price on a deal is whatever the seller chooses to show. The usual prices in this guide come from the maker's own store or from our monthly price check, so they are a fairer yardstick. A price-history tracker (camelcamelcamel and Keepa are two well-known ones for Amazon) shows what an item has actually sold for over the past months.

2. Check the exact model and generation

Older models are often the ones with the biggest discounts. An Apple Watch SE is not necessarily the SE 3, a Fire HD 8 may be the 32GB version, and Ring sells several battery doorbells with similar names. Match the model name in the listing title to the one in this guide.

3. Add up the monthly costs

For medical alerts, GPS trackers and cameras, the monthly plan can cost more in a year than the device itself. A big discount on the hardware means little if the plan is expensive or the deal locks you into a yearly term.

4. Check who is selling it

On Amazon, look under the Buy button for "Ships from" and "Sold by". Buying from Amazon or the maker's own store makes returns and warranty claims simpler than buying from an unknown third-party seller.

5. Know the return window before you buy a gift

If you are buying ahead for the holidays, check how long you have to return it. In 2025, Amazon said most items bought from November 1 to December 31 could be returned until January 31, with a shorter window for Apple products (January 15). Check this year's terms when you order, since they can change.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is Black Friday 2026?
Black Friday is Friday, November 27, 2026, the day after Thanksgiving (Thursday, November 26). Cyber Monday is November 30. Many retailers start their sales earlier in November, so it is worth checking prices on the picks above from the start of the month.
How do I know if a Black Friday price is a real deal?
Compare it with the usual price in this guide rather than the crossed-out "was" price, check that the model and generation match, and add up any monthly plan the product needs. A price-history tracker shows what the item has sold for in recent months. If the "deal" price is close to the usual price, there is no need to rush.
Do medical alert companies have Black Friday sales?
Yes, but on their own websites, not on Amazon. On October 25, 2026, Bay Alarm Medical's home page advertised up to 40% off plus free shipping and a free month on select plans, and Medical Guardian's advertised free activation and free shipping. Read the terms before you sign up: some offers only apply to yearly plans paid up front, or to new customers.
Is an AirTag a GPS tracker?
No. An AirTag finds things through Apple's Find My network, using nearby iPhones and iPads, not GPS. It works well for keys, wallets and bags, and needs an iPhone or iPad to set up and to find. For a parent who wanders, a real GPS tracker or a medical alert with GPS is the safer choice; our GPS tracker review compares them.
Which of these picks need a monthly subscription?
Medical alert systems and the LandAirSea 54 need a monthly plan. Ring and Blink cameras and doorbells work without one, but saving and replaying video needs a plan (Blink can also save to a USB stick with a Sync Module). The Apple Watch SE 3, AirTags, tablets, TV Ears and the Audien hearing aids have no monthly fee. The Fire TV Stick is free to use, but paid streaming services cost extra.
Is a TV listening headset an alternative to hearing aids?
No. A headset such as TV Ears helps with one thing, the TV. The NIDCD describes assistive listening devices like these as tools that can be used with or without hearing aids, not as a replacement for them. If your parent needs the TV much louder than before, a hearing check is worth booking too.
Who are over-the-counter hearing aids like the Audien for?
The FDA says OTC hearing aids are for adults 18 and older with perceived mild to moderate hearing loss. It advises seeing a doctor, preferably an ear, nose and throat specialist, first if there is ear pain, fluid or blood from the ear, sudden or fluctuating hearing changes, worse hearing in one ear, ringing in one ear, or severe dizziness. For more severe loss, a licensed hearing professional is the right place to start.
What if the gift is not right for my parent?
Check the return window before you buy. In 2025, Amazon said most items bought from November 1 to December 31 could be returned until January 31, with Apple products until January 15. Holiday terms can change each year, so confirm them on the order page. For medical alert plans, ask the provider about its trial period and cancellation terms before signing up.
