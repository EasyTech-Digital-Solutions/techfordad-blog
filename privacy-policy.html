Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026. Here's what this website collects, why, and how you can control it.

Overview

TechForDad ("we", "us", "our") publishes product reviews and buying guides for seniors and their caregivers. This policy explains what our website, and the services it uses, collect and how you can control it. You do not need an account to read the site. We only collect your email address if you choose to sign up for our newsletter.

Information We Collect

  • Newsletter signups. If you enter your email address in our "Free Guide" or newsletter form, we store it so we can send you the guide and occasional updates. The form is run by Mailchimp, which processes the address on our behalf. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • Usage data. Like most websites, our hosting and content delivery providers (GitHub Pages and Cloudflare) automatically log technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device type and the pages requested, so the site can be delivered and kept secure.
  • Analytics. We use Google Analytics to understand which guides are read, how visitors find them, and which product links are clicked, including clicks on links to Amazon. It records information such as pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser type, and the links you click, and it uses cookies (for example _ga) to recognise a browser between visits. We do not see what you buy. We also use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which measures traffic without cookies.
  • Advertising. This site shows ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners use cookies and similar technologies (for example __gads, __gpi, __eoi and _gcl_au) to show and measure ads, to limit how often you see the same ad and, unless you have opted out, to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. See "Advertising" below to opt out.
  • Cookies and local storage. Besides the analytics and advertising cookies above, Google's consent tool may store your choices in cookies such as FCCDCF, and our pages use your browser's local storage to remember for 14 days that you closed the gift announcement bar. Our content delivery network may also use cookies for performance and security.

How We Use Information

We use this information to run and improve the website, to send newsletter content to subscribers, to understand which guides are most helpful, and to pay for the site through advertising and affiliate commissions.

Advertising

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the Internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings, or opt out of many other companies' use of cookies for personalized ads at aboutads.info (in Canada, at youradchoices.ca). You can read how Google uses information from sites that use its services. Where the law requires it, Google's consent tool asks for your choice before cookies are used for advertising.

Analytics Choices

You can stop Google Analytics from recording your visits with Google's browser add-on, and you can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking cookies does not stop you reading the site.

Third-Party Links & Affiliate Programs

Many of our articles contain links to retailers such as Amazon and to product manufacturers. If you click one of these links and make a purchase, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. The retailer may set its own cookies so it can credit us for the referral. These sites have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them. See our Affiliate Disclosure for details.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with the service providers that help run the site: Mailchimp (newsletter emails, see the Intuit privacy statement), Google (analytics and advertising) and Cloudflare (delivery and security, see the Cloudflare privacy policy), along with our hosting provider, GitHub. Advertising partners such as Google may receive cookie identifiers and browsing information as described under "Advertising". Our providers may process information in the United States and other countries.

How Long We Keep It

We keep newsletter addresses until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them. Analytics data is kept for the period set in our Google Analytics account.

Your Choices and Rights

You can unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time using the link in any email we send. Depending on where you live (for example Canada, California, or the European Economic Area and United Kingdom), you may have the right to ask what information we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, and to withdraw your consent. To make a request, contact us at hello@techfordad.com and we will respond. You can also use the advertising and analytics choices described above. Canadian residents can learn about their privacy rights from the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Children

This website is written for adults. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy? Reach us at hello@techfordad.com or visit our Contact page.
