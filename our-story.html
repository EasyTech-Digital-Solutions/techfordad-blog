About This Project

TechForDad is published by EasyTech Vancouver. We write buying guides for adult children and caregivers choosing technology for an aging parent: medical alerts, phones, tablets, hearing aids and home safety devices, with separate guides for Canada and the US.

Most tech reviews are written for people who already like technology. Ours focus on what matters to an 80 year old with weaker eyesight, hearing or dexterity, and to the family member setting things up.

How We Research

Every guide follows the same process:

  • Manufacturer details. We read the specifications, plan terms and prices on each maker's own website.
  • Published reviews. We look at expert reviews and verified buyer reviews for problems that come up again and again.
  • Accessibility guidance. We weigh features that matter for older users, such as screen size, volume, button size and ease of setup, using published guidance from health and accessibility organizations.
  • Monthly price checks. We recheck prices and models every month against the maker's store. When a product is discontinued or a claim turns out to be wrong, we update the guide and add a dated note.

We do not physically test every product, and we do not claim personal experience we do not have. We use software tools, including AI, to help gather specifications and prices and to draft guides.

Editorial Independence
