# Architecture: Gift Guides Section

## Site structure
* Domain: techfordad.com (existing domain, no new domain or subdomain)
* Hub page: techfordad.com/gift guides (central landing page)
* Subpages: individual guide posts nested under the hub, each targeting one specific gift theme or budget

## Navigation
* Add "Gift Guides" as a top level item in the main site navigation
* Hub page links out to every individual guide post
* Every guide post links back to the hub page

## Homepage integration
* Featured section or card block placed a few scrolls down from the top
* Section prominence increases in October through December, and again around Mother's Day and Father's Day
* Outside peak season, the section can shrink or move lower on the page, but should stay linked somewhere on the homepage

## Internal linking
* Existing tech review posts get contextual links pointing into relevant Gift Guides posts (example: a tablet review links to a related gift guide)
* This spreads authority from already indexed pages into new Gift Guides content

## Tracking
* Create a distinct Amazon Associates tracking ID for this section
* Use that tracking ID's links specifically within Gift Guides posts, separate from the default site wide link
* This allows the Tracking ID report in Amazon Associates to show performance for this section separately from general traffic

## Content hosting
* Standard blog post format, same CMS and theme as the rest of the site, no separate platform or hosting needed
