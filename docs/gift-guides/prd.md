# PRD: Gift Guides Section (TechForDad)

## Goal
Add a Gift Guides content section under the existing techfordad.com domain to capture holiday and occasion based search traffic, and convert it into Amazon Associates affiliate commissions.

## Background
* TechForDad is a senior focused affiliate blog, currently monetized with AdSense and Amazon Associates
* Low indexing and domain authority are known issues
* New content under the existing domain ranks faster than a new domain or subdomain would

## Target audience
* Adult children shopping for aging parents
* Grandchildren shopping for grandparents
* Caregivers looking for practical, senior friendly gift ideas

## Success metrics
* Number of Gift Guides pages indexed by Google
* Organic traffic to the Gift Guides hub and subpages
* Click through rate on Amazon links inside gift content
* Orders and commission tied to a dedicated Gift Guides tracking ID

## Scope
* One central hub page: "Gift Guides"
* Individual guide posts under the hub (examples: gifts under $50, tech gifts seniors actually use, gifts for grandparents who have everything)
* Seasonal timing tied to Christmas, Mother's Day, Father's Day, and Grandparents Day
* Cross links from existing tech review posts into relevant guides

## Out of scope for this phase
* New domain or subdomain
* Non Amazon affiliate programs
* Non English content

## Key decisions already made
* Content lives under the existing techfordad.com domain, not a new domain or subdomain
* Gift Guides appears in the main navigation menu
* Homepage gets a featured section for Gift Guides, more prominent from October through December and around Mother's Day and Father's Day
* A dedicated tracking ID will be created to measure this section separately from general site traffic
