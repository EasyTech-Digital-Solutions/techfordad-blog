# Memory: Gift Guides Section

## Site context
* Parent site: techfordad.com, a senior focused affiliate blog
* Monetized with AdSense and Amazon Associates
* Known issue: low indexing and domain authority on the main site
* Amazon Associates account: techfordad0b-20, US program, tax status complete for both US and Canada, Canadian bank account added for direct deposit

## Why this section exists
* Seasonal gift content (Christmas, Mother's Day, Father's Day, Grandparents Day) tends to spike affiliate clicks
* Adding it as content under the existing domain, instead of a new domain or subdomain, was chosen to use existing domain authority instead of starting SEO from zero

## Key decisions made so far
* Location: subfolder or section under techfordad.com, not a new domain or subdomain
* Navigation: Gift Guides appears in the main nav at all times
* Homepage: featured block, more prominent during peak seasons, smaller but still present off season
* Tracking: separate Amazon Associates tracking ID for this section, kept apart from the site's general tracking
* Structure: one central hub page linking out to individual guide posts, with guide posts linking back to the hub
