# Whole-site SEO and content sweep, 2026-10-05

Scope: every indexable page (72 files audited: articles, guides, gift guides, info pages, hubs, stubs), content accuracy and technical SEO.
Method: a scripted audit (metadata, headings, images, links, schema, claims wording, sitemap, external link health) plus manual
verification of health, tax, insurance and government-program claims against sources that could be opened.

## Fixed

**Accuracy and transparency**
- Canada tax and program claims corrected from CRA / provincial pages: blood pressure monitors and personal response systems (Lifeline) are listed by the
  CRA as expenses you cannot claim; Ontario's Seniors' Home Safety Tax Credit covered 2021 and 2022 only; Alberta SHARP funds basic repairs, not security
  upgrades; removed an unverified "cell phone tax deduction" and four unverified provincial phone-subsidy bullets; phone page now matches the verified plans page.
- Insurance discount figures replaced by Triple-I and Insurance Bureau of Canada wording (no invented 5-20% / 2-5% / $75-$300 ranges).
- Statistics now sourced or attributed: caregiver counts (AARP/NAC 2025: 59 million caring for adults, 63 million family caregivers), sleep apnea (NHLBI),
  falls (CDC), hearing loss (StatCan), maker claims labelled as the maker's ("Oticon says", "ELEHEAR says").
- Removed invented experience ("caregivers we've spoken with", "we've seen families", "in our experience") and unsupported superlatives
  ("industry-leading", "no other tracker comes close", "the only tracker we can recommend").
- Product names: "HearCARE Canada" is "HearCANADA".
- About page: added Who Publishes TechForDad (EasyTech Vancouver, founder background and credentials as published by EasyTech, separate paid tech support disclosed),
  Health and Medical Topics, and Corrections.
- Health notice merged into the affiliate box at the top of 18 medical pages (generated by scripts/build_health_note.py).

**Technical SEO**
- robots.txt no longer blocks /page/, /author/, /category/ (their stubs rely on noindex, which Google cannot see when blocked); regression test added.
- Product schema currency (USD vs CAD) fixed; FAQPage schema is now generated from the visible FAQ (scripts/build_faq_schema.py) and repairs broken blocks.
- Article schema: image on all articles, guides and gift guides; publisher parentOrganization with EasyTech sameAs profiles.
- Social sharing: raster default image (images/og-default.png; social platforms ignore SVG), summary_large_image, twitter:image/title/description on every page.
- Home page layout shift 0.135 to 0 (gift block ordering moved from JS to CSS).
- Titles lengthened where short (About, Contact); overstated meta descriptions corrected; sitemap lastmod, dateModified and visible "Updated" dates moved only on pages whose content changed.
- External links: 175 of 182 respond; the rest are bot-blocks (ADT, Kobo, CRTC, heart.org, optout.aboutads.info) or a certificate error from here (news.gov.bc.ca). Check those in a browser.
